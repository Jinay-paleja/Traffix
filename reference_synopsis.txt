Abstract 
 
In today's urban lifestyle, students, bachelors, and working professionals often face challenges in maintaining a healthy and affordable diet due to limited access to home-cooked food. Simultaneously, numerous skilled home chefs possess exceptional culinary abilities but lack a proper platform to reach potential customers. To address this gap, Ghar Ka Khaana has been developed as a centralized webbased platform that connects individuals seeking nutritious, home-cooked meals with verified local home chefs. The system allows users to browse menus, place orders, schedule deliveries, and make secure online payments with ease. For home chefs, it offers an opportunity to showcase their culinary skills, manage orders, and earn income. The platform ensures hygiene, affordability, and convenience, providing a sustainable solution for those living away from home.  
 
By automating and digitalizing the homemade meal service process, Ghar Ka Khaana fosters a culture of healthy eating, supports small-scale entrepreneurs, and strengthens the local economy. It promotes environmental and social sustainability by encouraging home-based food production and reducing dependence on commercial fast-food outlets. The system's modular architecture also allows for future scalability - including real-time delivery tracking, mobile app integration, AI-based meal recommendations, and partnerships with hostels and local vendors.
Chapter 1 Introduction 
Ghar Ka Khaana is a web-based platform designed to connect students, bachelors, and working professionals with verified home chefs offering affordable, hygienic, and freshly cooked meals. It bridges the gap between individuals seeking healthy food and home-based cooks looking to expand their reach. The system provides role-based access for Users, Chefs, and Admins - where users can browse meals, subscribe to tiffin plans, and make payments; chefs can manage profiles, menus, and orders; and admins handle verification, feedback, and overall platform management. The platform ensures efficiency, transparency, and scalability. By digitalizing homemade food services, Ghar Ka Khaana promotes healthy eating, supports local entrepreneurs, and contributes to a sustainable, community-driven food ecosystem. 
1.1 Motivation 
Students, working professionals, and bachelors living away from home often face challenges in maintaining a healthy diet due to time constraints and limited access to homemade food. On the other hand, many skilled home cooks prepare quality meals but lack visibility and a reliable way to reach customers. Ghar Ka Khaana aims to bridge this gap by creating a simple, community-based food delivery platform that benefits both customers and home cooks. 
1.2 Problem Analysis 
Existing food delivery apps mainly promote restaurants, not home kitchens. Home cooks struggle to reach customers, and users cannot easily find verified local meal providers. The main issues are lack of visibility, trust, and a structured system for menu listings, subscriptions, and order management. 
1.3 Objectives 
• To connect home chefs with students and bachelors seeking homemade food. 
• To provide verified chef profiles with menus and pricing. 
• To enable users to order or subscribe to meal plans by location and cuisine. 
• To offer secure login, order tracking, and feedback options. 
 
 
 
1.4 Scope 
Web-based platform with registration, menu listing, subscriptions, advance payments and feedback. Out of scope: real-time delivery tracking, large-scale logistics management and mobile app support for future phases. 

 
  	Chapter 2  	Literature Review
 
1.1 Related Work 
    Automation and digitization in the food service industry have become increasingly significant        for improving customer experience, operational efficiency, and accessibility. Jadhav and      More [1] discuss the development of web-based food ordering systems that streamline      interactions between customers and food providers. Their findings emphasize the importance         of digital interfaces, real-time order tracking, and efficient data management - principles         that directly inform the design of Ghar Ka Khaana, which connects users with verified home          chefs through a centralized online platform. 
Sharma et al. [2] explore online tiffin and homemade food delivery solutions aimed at students and working professionals. Their research highlights the growing demand for healthy, home-cooked meals and the challenges faced in ensuring affordability, hygiene, and timely delivery. Ghar Ka Khaana adopts these insights by implementing a structured subscription-based model that provides accessible and nutritious meal options through verified local chefs. 
Patel and Nair [3] examine role-based food delivery management systems, underscoring the need for different access levels for users, service providers, and administrators. Their study demonstrates how role-based authorization enhances security and operational control. 
Inspired by this, Ghar Ka Khaana incorporates dedicated dashboards for Users, Chefs, and Admins to ensure transparency, accountability, and efficient coordination. 
Kaur and Bhatia [4] analyze feedback-driven online food platforms, emphasizing how customer reviews and ratings improve service quality and trust. Integrating similar mechanisms, Ghar Ka Khaana includes a feedback and rating module that helps maintain service standards and promotes continuous improvement among home chefs. 
Gupta and Iyer [5] discuss the application of cloud databases and web technologies in scalable online service systems. Their research supports the use of flexible, non-relational databases for handling large and dynamic datasets. Ghar Ka Khaana leverages MongoDB for efficient data management, scalability, and seamless integration with the platform's backend. 
Collectively, these studies highlight that automation, role-based management, feedback integration, and scalable architectures are essential elements in building efficient digital food delivery systems. Drawing from these works, Ghar Ka Khaana incorporates these principles to create a reliable, secure, and community-driven platform that connects customers with local home chefs while promoting healthy and affordable eating habits. 
1.2 Existing System 
The current food delivery ecosystem is dominated by platforms like Swiggy and Zomato, which focus mainly on restaurant-based meals. While convenient, they often lack affordability, personalization, and the homely touch that many consumers seek. They also offer limited opportunities for local home chefs to participate and earn through digital means. 
Local tiffin services and small vendors address this demand for homemade food but rely on manual or WhatsApp-based ordering. WhatsApp/Facebook groups allow quick local communication but lack verified chefs, menu consistency, order tracking, and structured records. Even small-scale NGO or community initiatives provide local food options but lack scalable digital infrastructure for discovery, subscription plans, and secure payments. 
These systems lack automation, order tracking, and secure digital payments, making management inefficient and error-prone. 
Some services attempt partial digitization using Google Forms or Sheets, which simplify data entry but lack role-based access, chef verification, and privacy controls. Meanwhile, ERP systems and other enterprise tools are too complex and expensive for small home-based kitchens, limiting adoption. 
Many home chefs still handle orders manually through calls or messages, making scalability and performance tracking difficult. 
These limitations highlight the need for a dedicated platform like Ghar Ka Khaana, which integrates automation, verified chef profiles, subscription management, secure payments, and real-time order tracking - offering an efficient, transparent, and user-friendly solution for connecting consumers with authentic homemade meals. 
Chapter 3 Proposed System 
3.1 Functionalities of the System 
The proposed system, Ghar Ka Khaana - Home Food Delivery Platform, is designed to digitize, streamline, and automate the process of ordering and delivering homemade meals. The platform ensures efficiency, transparency, and convenience for both customers and home chefs. It provides a modular structure with clearly defined functionalities for each user role, including customers, chefs, and administrators, enabling smooth order management, secure transactions, and reliable delivery tracking. 
1. Authentication & Authorization Module 
? Secure user registration and login for customers, chefs, and admins. 
? Role-based access control to ensure proper permissions. 
? Password hashing for data protection. 
? Profile management for updating personal details and preferences. 
2. Meal & Menu Management Module 
? Home chefs can add, edit, or remove meals from their menus. 
? Upload meal images, descriptions, ingredients, and prices. 
? Categorization of meals (veg, non-veg, special, combo, etc). 
? Customers can view menus and meal details before ordering. 
3. Order & Subscription Management Module 
? Customers can place single orders or subscribe to weekly/monthly tiffin plans. 
? Order tracking from preparation to delivery. 
? Cancellation and modification options before order confirmation. ? Automated status updates for chefs and customers. 
4. Payment & Transaction Module 
? Secure online payments through integrated gateways. 
? Order confirmation only after successful payment. 
? Transaction history available for customers and chefs. 
5. Feedback & Rating Module 
? Customers can rate meals and provide feedback. 
? Chef performance monitoring based on ratings. 
? Admins can review feedback to maintain quality standards. 
6. Admin Management Module 
? Admin dashboard to monitor users, orders, and system activity. 
? Chef verification and approval before account activation. 
? Analytics for sales trends, customer engagement, and chef performance. 
7. Notification & Communication Module 
? Email and in-platform notifications for order updates and subscriptions. 
? Alerts for payment confirmation, order delivery, and feedback reminders. ? Messaging support between customers and chefs (if enabled). 8. UI/UX Module 
? Responsive, mobile-friendly interface. 
? Clear role-based navigation and dashboards. 
? Modern design using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for smooth interactions. ? Emphasis on accessibility and ease of use for all user roles.

3.2 Proposed Approach and Details 
The Ghar Ka Khaana platform consists of a modular and scalable architecture, integrating backend APIs with a React-based frontend. The backend manages user authentication, order processing, menu management, and delivery tracking. The frontend ensures a seamless, responsive experience for students and bachelors, offering easy navigation through menus, personalized meal plans, and real- time order updates. 
 
The proposed 3-tier architecture of the Ghar Ka Khaana platform consists of: The frontend (presentation layer), developed using React, provides a responsive and user-friendly interface for students and bachelors to browse menus, place orders, and track deliveries. The backend (application layer), built with Node.js and Express, handles authentication, order processing, menu management, and notifications. The database layer, implemented using MongoDB, efficiently stores user details, meal data, and order histories, ensuring smooth data retrieval and consistency across the system.

2. Modular Architecture: Separate modules for users, menus, orders, and deliveries ensure easy scalability. 
3. Complete Digital Workflow: Handles everything from ordering to delivery seamlessly online. 
4. Analytics Dashboard: Tracks popular dishes, peak times, and delivery performance. 
5. Eco-Friendly & Student-Focused: Promotes sustainable, affordable, home-cooked meals for students and bachelors.

Chapter 4 Implementation Details and Results 
The implementation phase of Ghar Ka Khaana focused on developing a fully functional web platform based on the proposed design and architecture. This chapter highlights the technologies, development process, and key outcomes. The system ensures smooth interaction between customers, home chefs, and administrators while maintaining security, reliability, and user-friendliness for an efficient homemade meal experience. 
4.1  Technology Tech Stack 
Backend Technologies 
1. Runtime Environment:  
• Java (JDK 17)- Used to build server-side logic and handle core business operations. 
2. Framework:  
• Java Servlets with JSP (Java Sever Pages)- For processing client requests and generating dynamic web content. 
3. Database: 
• MongoDB- Used for efficient storage and retrieval of user profiles, chef details, menus and orders. 
4. Database Connectivity: 
• JDBC (Java Database Connectivity)- Enables seamless communication between the Java backend and the MongoDB database. 
 
 
 
5. Authentication & Security: 
• Session Management- Maintains user login states securely. 
• Password Encryption- Implements SHA-256 hashing via Java's MessageDigest for secure password handling. 
 
6. Input Validation & Sanitization: 
• Prevents injection attacks and ensures data integrity. 
 
7. File Handling: 
• Apache Commons File Upload (Servlet File Upload)- Manages profile image and menu images upload efficiently. 
 
8. Email Integration: 
• JavaMail API- Sends order confirmations, feedback acknowledgments, and chef notifications automatically. 
 
9. Configuration Files: 
• Store database credentials, environment variables, and other sensitive configurations securely.
       Frontend Technologies 
 
1. Markup & Styling: 
• HTML5and CSS3- For creating structured, responsive and visually appealing layouts. 
2. Interactivity: 
• JavaScript (ES6)- For dynamic elements, form validation and real time interactions. 
3. Responsive Design: 
• Flexbox and Grid systems for mobile- friendly layouts. 
4. UI Enhancements: 
• Custom CSS animations for menus, dashboards and feedback forms. 
5. HTTP Client: 
• Fetch API to communicate with backend rest endpoints.

Chapter 5 Sustainability Analysis and Testing 
The Ghar Ka Khaana platform is designed not only as a functional food delivery system but also as a sustainable, scalable, and maintainable solution for connecting customers with home chefs. This chapter highlights the system's sustainability aspects, testing approaches, and performance outcomes, ensuring that the platform operates efficiently under various conditions while supporting long-term growth and community impact. 
 
 
    5.1 Sustainability Analysis 
The sustainability of Ghar Ka Khaana is evaluated from technical, operational environments and social perspectives: 
1. Technical Sustainability: 
? Modular Architecture: Separate modules for authentication, order management, payments, and feedback enable easy maintenance and scalability. 
? Database Scalability: MongoDB supports large user bases, meal listings, and transactions without performance issues. 
2. Operational Sustainability: 
? Automation: Simplifies order handling, chef verification, and subscription management, reducing manual work. 
? User Convenience: Real-time updates on meal status and subscriptions improve overall experience. 
3. Environmental Sustainability: 
? Paperless System: Fully digital ordering and subscription reduce paper waste. ? Resource Efficiency: Optimized backend and lightweight frontend minimize server energy consumption. 
4. Social and Economic Sustainability: 
? Empowerment: Supports local home chefs by providing an online marketplace. ? Community Impact: Promotes healthy, home-cooked meals for students and professionals. 
 
5.2 Testing Methodology 
1. White Box Testing: 
• Focused on internal code logic, loops, and data flow within modules. 
• Verified logic paths, conditions, and function outputs for backend APIs. 
• Ensured optimized and bug-free algorithms for order and payment handling. 
 
2. Black Box Testing: 
• Tested the system without internal code visibility. 
 
• Checked input-output behavior such as order submission, login validation, and payment confirmation. 
 
• Ensured system behaves correctly under valid and invalid user inputs. 
 
 
 
 
 
Chapter 6 Conclusion 
The Ghar Ka Khaana system provides a reliable and user-friendly platform for students and bachelors to conveniently access fresh, homemade food. By connecting home chefs with customers through a digital interface, the system promotes healthy eating and supports local home-based food providers. The platform ensures smooth functionality through secure authentication, efficient order management, and real-time tracking features. 
Comprehensive testing, including unit, integration, functional, white box, and black box testing, confirmed the system's performance, security, and usability. The solution reduces dependency on fast food, minimizes manual processes, and enhances overall user satisfaction. Designed for scalability and sustainability, Ghar Ka Khaana successfully bridges the gap between homemade meals and modern convenience, contributing to a healthier and smarter lifestyle for urban students and working individuals.
